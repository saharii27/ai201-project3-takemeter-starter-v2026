sourced_claim: makes a point about the job market or tech industry and includes evidence a stranger could check from the post itself: a link, a direct quote, or a specific named report or announcement
hot_take: a confident claim or piece of news about the industry with nothing a stranger could check, including hearsay, vague sources, and claims backed only by the poster's own experience
personal_story: shares the poster's own situation or feelings without arguing a point about the wider job market
