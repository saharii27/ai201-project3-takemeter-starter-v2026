# TakeMeter notes

## Community
r/cscareerquestions (public, no login needed)

## Candidate distinctions (each one something I can point at)
1. Refers to a source or study, but gives no number, link, or quote. (Is it checkable, or just name-dropped?)

2. States a claim or piece of news with no support offered.

3. Shares the poster's own situation and feeling, not an argument about the field.



## Standout posts
- "Harvard just released another study about AI productivity in software engineering and it found there is no increase in productivity from using Claude code." → why it stood out: Points to a specific study, which makes it feel substantial, but there's no figure, link, or detail, so I can't check it from the post alone.

- "Cto is planning on firing all junior and mid level devs" → why it stood out: Big claim, stated flatly, with nothing behind it. No context on how the poster knows.

- "I'm a doomer and I finally got a pretty cushy job. I feel so fortunate." → why it stood out: About the poster's own experience and feelings. It isn't arguing anything about the job market.
